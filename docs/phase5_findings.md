# Phase 5 — Sales Analytics: Key Findings

Full query-by-query results (SQL + output tables) are in
[`reports/phase5_sales_analytics_report.md`](../reports/phase5_sales_analytics_report.md),
generated by running [`sql/05_sales_analytics.sql`](../sql/05_sales_analytics.sql)
against the Phase 4 database. This document summarizes the findings and
labels each as a **Fact** (directly computed) or an **Observation**
(pattern noticed in the facts — still evidence-based, not speculation).
Business recommendations are deferred to Phase 13.

## Overall Performance

* **Fact:** Total revenue across the full dataset (2016-01-01 to
  2021-02-20) is **$55,755,479.59**, from 26,326 orders and 11,887
  distinct purchasing customers. Average order value is **$2,117.89**.
* **Fact:** Revenue grew every full year from 2016 ($6.95M) through 2019
  ($18.26M peak), then declined in 2020 (-49.11% YoY, $9.29M). 2021 shows
  only 2 months of data ($1.04M) and is **not comparable** to full-year
  figures.
* **Observation:** The 2020 decline is consistent with pandemic-era
  disruption timing, but this dataset contains no explanatory fields (no
  marketing spend, no macro indicators) — the *cause* of the 2020 drop
  cannot be established from this data alone.
* **Fact:** Month-over-month revenue is highly volatile, with recurring
  sharp dips in March–April of most years (e.g. -67% to -83% swings) and
  a consistent December revenue peak every year in the dataset — a
  seasonal pattern, not a one-off.

## Product Performance

* **Fact:** The top 10 products by revenue are all in **Computers** and
  **TV and Video**, led by "WWI Desktop PC2.33 X2330 Black" ($505,450).
* **Fact:** Top-by-quantity and top-by-revenue lists mostly overlap
  (dominated by Computers), but "WWI Desktop PC1.80 E1800 White" is a
  top-2 unit seller ($123,686 revenue) despite ranking well outside the
  revenue top 10 — a lower-priced, high-volume product.
* **Fact:** 25 of 2,517 catalog products (~1%) have **zero recorded
  sales** in this dataset.
* **Fact:** The bottom 10 products by revenue are all low-cost
  accessories (USB cables, case fans) from the Southridge Video and
  Litware brands, each generating under $40 in total revenue.
* **Fact:** **Computers** is the leading category at **34.62%** of total
  revenue ($19.3M), followed by Home Appliances (19.36%) and Cameras and
  camcorders (11.69%). **Games and Toys** is the smallest category at
  1.30% of revenue.
* **Fact:** Every product category grew every full year from 2016–2019,
  then declined in 2020 — the 2020 downturn was broad-based across all 8
  categories (range: -43.9% to -59.0% YoY), not concentrated in one area.

## Profitability

* **Fact:** Product-level profit margins in this dataset cluster around
  a small number of fixed values (~49%, ~54%, ~66.9%) rather than varying
  continuously per product — confirmed directly against `Products.csv`
  (30 distinct rounded margin values across 2,517 products, with three
  values accounting for the large majority of products). This means
  margin differences across products mostly reflect which of a few
  pricing tiers a product belongs to, not individual product economics.
* **Fact:** At the category level, profit margin is fairly narrow across
  the board (54.7%–61.0%), with **Music, Movies and Audio Books** having
  the highest margin (60.98%) despite being a mid-small revenue category,
  and **Games and Toys** the lowest (54.73%).

## Geography & Channel

* **Fact:** The **United States** generates 53.58% of total revenue
  ($29.87M) from customers, more than the next three countries (UK,
  Germany, Canada) combined.
* **Fact:** At the state/region level, **California** ($3.06M) and
  **Texas** ($2.25M) lead among U.S. states; **Ontario** ($1.98M) is the
  top Canadian province.
* **Fact:** **In-store sales account for 79.55%** of revenue ($44.35M)
  vs. **20.45% online** ($11.40M). Average order value is similar across
  channels ($2,137.82 in-store vs. $2,043.79 online) — the channel gap is
  about order volume, not order size.
* **Fact:** By store-side geography, the single "Online" pseudo-store
  ($11.40M) outsells every individual country's physical stores except
  the United States — it is effectively the company's second-largest
  "location."

## Data Caveats Carried Forward

* All geography analysis above uses **customer location**, not store
  location, except where explicitly labeled "store-side" — see
  `docs/database_schema.md` and the upcoming KPI framework (Phase 10) for
  why both exist and when each is used.
* 2021 figures are partial-year and should not be compared directly to
  full years without normalization.
